SnapCard prototypes

Three working directions for the business card scanner. Each one includes the scan flow, review form, contacts list and a full admin panel. Open on a phone and use "Add to Home Screen" to test the PWA feel.

1 · Cobalt

Light, professional, Salesforce-adjacent. Sidebar on desktop, tab bar on mobile.

2 · Nightshift

Dark, technical, field-ops feel. Icon rail on desktop, mono data styling.

3 · Signal

Warm minimal with a guided 3-step wizard: capture, review, saved.

Prototype note: card reading and Salesforce sync are simulated. Saved contacts persist in the browser via localStorage, separately per layout.
